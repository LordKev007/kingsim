Coach Bob Analysis

Key moments

Coach Bob evaluates both armies by the same standard, separately from the result. Bob looks through the initiative order to each unit's next activation, projects enemy reach and damage, checks for hanging pieces and safer legal finishes, and reconciles the review with the actual battle outcome. Coach Suggestions use Bob's live evaluator and are recorded as assistance, but are not themselves a scoring penalty. The score is still a King's Grid heuristic evaluation, not an infallible solved-game oracle.
